Features

Designed for your fingers,
not a mouse.

Every interaction is rethought from first principles for a touch-native experience.

✋
Direct Touch Control
Tap exactly where you want to click. No floating cursor to drag around — just your finger on the screen. It's the interaction model remote desktop has always needed.
Zero Friction
🖥️
Multi-Monitor Support
Swipe left and right to move between your Mac's displays like flipping through pages. Each monitor gets its own canvas — full resolution, full control.
Multiple Displays
📡
Auto Discovery
AirDesk finds your Mac automatically over WiFi using Bonjour. No IP addresses to type, no manual setup. Just open the app and tap your Mac to connect.
Bonjour Powered

How It Works

Three steps.
Zero hassle.

Get connected in under a minute — seriously.

1
Install AirDesk on your Mac
Download and run the lightweight AirDesk companion app on your Mac. It runs quietly in the menu bar, ready whenever you need it.
2
Open AirDesk on iPhone
Launch AirDesk on your iPhone or iPad while on the same WiFi network. Your Mac appears automatically — no configuration required.
3
Tap your Mac to connect
Tap your Mac in the device list and you're instantly viewing and controlling your desktop. Swipe between monitors, tap to click, pinch to zoom.

Why AirDesk

Built different,
by design.

Other remote desktop apps were built for mice. AirDesk was built for your thumb.

✓
Native SwiftUI throughout Smooth 120Hz animations, proper haptics, and system-consistent UI that feels at home on iOS.
✓
No account, no cloud Your connection stays between your iPhone and your Mac. Nothing leaves your local network.
✓
Direct touch — not cursor mode Competitors give you a tiny cursor to chase. AirDesk maps your touch directly to screen coordinates.
✓
Per-monitor display paging Each Mac display gets its own swipeable page — no zoomed-out overview to squint at.

Requirements

What you'll need.

AirDesk keeps it simple — any modern Mac and iPhone will do.

💻
Mac (Companion App)
  • macOS Ventura 13.0 or later
  • AirDesk companion installed
  • Same WiFi network as your iPhone
  • Screen Recording permission granted
📱
iPhone / iPad
  • iOS / iPadOS 16.0 or later
  • AirDesk app from TestFlight
  • Same WiFi network as your Mac
  • Any iPhone or iPad (no special hardware)

Remote access from anywhere — coming soon

AirDesk is adding Cloudflare Tunnel support so you can securely reach your Mac from anywhere in the world — not just your home network. No port forwarding, no VPN.
